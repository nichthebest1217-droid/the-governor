Prom was yet another memorable night. Although the weather did not cooperate, we made do and still made it a great night for the Govs community to come together. Due to the rain, the pictures were moved inside on campus. The library and Phillips became the hot spots, with parents, teachers, and promgoers all crammed in to take as many pictures as they could.

Promposals were a hit again this year, and people got really creative. From costumes to full kidnapping setups, everyone found a way to make theirs memorable. Some of the most notable costumes included The Lorax, Curious George, and even a prisoner involved in one of the kidnapping-themed promposals. We also had someone recreate a viral meme with a makeshift casket, which definitely got people talking. If you wanted to keep up with all of them, you had to turn to Instagram.

The venue was the State Room in Boston, only about a 45-minute drive from campus. The view of the city skyline with the harbor in the background was amazing and was used as a backdrop for more pictures. Students quickly found their seats at tables with their friends. Not long after, the chatter started: “Where’s the food?” Lines eventually formed around the buffet. When asking Brodie ‘26 about the food, he said, “The food was dust; the only good thing was the mac and cheese. They needed more protein.” There were plenty of mixed reviews about the food. The one heard the most was the shortage of chicken. When paying $130 for the ticket, the hope would be not to run out of chicken. On the positive side, students only had good things to say about the mac and cheese

The chicken situation didn’t stop people from starting to crowd the dance floor. In the blink of an eye, the dance floor was packed, and if you were in the middle of it all, you did not have much room to yourself. When asking Andrew ‘26 about the music, he said, “There were not a lot of known dance songs.” Though many were unhappy with the music selection, it did not stop the dancing or the excitement. The dancing was so intense that one promgoer ripped his pants while doing a split. By the end of the night, all the dancers were sweaty and exhausted, but jazzed about the fun they had.

Prom, as always, was a memorable event. It is a great way to bring the community together at the end of the school year.
