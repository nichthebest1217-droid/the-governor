After a rough 0-8 season last year, the Governor’s Academy football team is already showing major signs of improvement this fall. After an exciting scrimmage win over Brooks, the team aims to build on that performance and keep dominating the challenging ISL competition. With many recruits and a new approach to game-time preparation, the Govs football team hopes to succeed this season.

Many players have given positive feedback about the new practice and film schedule. Coach Gardner Cousins ‘20 said that “allowing the players to watch the film together and then dissect each play on the whiteboard has allowed the team to translate more of what they learn from film onto the field.” The team plans to build on the established gritty culture that has led to past success.

New recruits like Wyatt Ayers ‘29, Jakob Witt ‘28, Chase Dowling ‘28, Jaden Wilbon ‘29, and Brayden Fowler ‘28 have helped the football program improve tremendously. As additions to the D-line, linebacker core, and defensive back group, these new recruits have already demonstrated an immediate impact on the program.

Captain Owen Denio ‘27 said, “The biggest thing that's going to be different is the attitude around every game and practice. We’ve been coming out with a lot of energy, and it seems to be a better overall vibe on the team, and it seems like more of a brotherhood as opposed to last year.”

These new recruits have impacted Govs football on the field, and their commitment to the team and the leadership they bring has also reinforced the program’s culture. Brandon Hennessey ‘27 said “I think that being a community where we all see each other as equal and we don't have one person thinking that they’re all that means something to the team”. All in all there is a lot of excitement around the Govs football program this year, and with students and parents eager to see the results of these changes.

This week the Governors will get another chance to demonstrate their talents against Middlesex in which players like Owen Denio ‘27, Brandon Hennessey ‘27, and Tyler Kasztejna ‘28 are expected to dominate and lead the Governors to a big win. Overall the team seems more complete and more united than ever before and we’re all excited to see how this year's Governors football team will perform.
