Graduation brings excitement, uncertainty, and a sense of freedom for seniors leaving high school. With classes, sports, and exams finally ending, many students are looking towards a freedom-filled summer that will be both a break from school and college preparation. To see what this looks like for the Class of 2026, an anonymous survey was sent out asking seniors to share their post-grad plans. The responses included a little bit of everything, from internships and jobs to travel, rest, and fun.

Some students are planning on taking on internships or career-focused programs. For some, this summer is all about preparing for the future. Internships and training programs are a popular choice for students looking to gain experience before college begins. One senior shared that they are doing an “EMT training course during the week and working at a restaurant on weekends,” while another said, “I’m planning to go to the gym, complete an internship, and get myself prepared for college.” These plans show that even during summer break, many students are still focused on growth and preparation, using their time to build skills, explore interests, and take meaningful steps toward their future careers.

Many seniors are spending part of their summer working, whether it’s for extra cash or just something to do. From restaurants and retail to lifeguarding, seniors are stepping into all kinds of different jobs. One student described working at a marina for the third summer, helping with boats and assisting customers. One senior is coming back to Govs over the summer, working with Govs Plus, where she will be teaching classes and mentoring students. One student is planning on caddying, while another plans to work at a boutique in Nantucket. Whether it’s saving money for college or just gaining independence, these jobs are a big part of many students’ summers.

Travel and relaxation are just as important, with students planning trips to other continents, going to concerts, heading to the beach, and spending time with loved ones. People are traveling to a wide variety of places, including Cape Cod, Nantucket, Japan, Florida, Greece, and Italy. One student wrote that they were just planning on sleeping, saying, “Let a man rest.” After four years of hard work at Govs, no one can argue with that. One student even wrote that “I’m going to become homeless exploring the world… then come back at the end of the summer and become a billionaire.” A plan that ambitious definitely captures the spirit of senior summer!

In the end, this summer is all about transition. Whether seniors are working, traveling, interning, or just relaxing, the choices they make reflect not only what they want to do, but who they are becoming. Each plan offers insight into their priorities, ambitions, and personalities at this turning point in their lives. Before everyone heads off in different directions for college, this summer will be a time to make memories, try new things, and enjoy a well-deserved break.
