If you walked into the Remis Art Gallery in Wilkie on Friday, September 18th, you would be greeted by many members of the Govs community coming together to appreciate art submissions from staff across campus. Dr. O’s beautiful violin music fills the room, and you see an assortment of amazing art from staff across campus. Photography, paintings, sculptures, and even embroidery pieces cover the walls and stand on tables around the room.

The Staff Art Show gave us a unique opportunity to see the different talents of many Govs staff members. The teachers in the art department, as well as the Kaiser proctors, worked to provide a platform for anyone on the staff to show off their creativity. Kaiser proctor Lana Hardin ‘27 said she believed the event was worthwhile: “I think it really represents how tight of a community we have here at Govs.” Ms. Fitz, another organizer of the art show, said: “It was great to see everyone coming to appreciate what all the artists created.” She hopes to have more staff featured in the future.

From Ms. Roberto’s expressive charcoal portraits to Mr. Ogden’s reluctantly submitted poems, the show featured a wide variety of creations and creators. The art show gave the students a chance to see things the staff do outside of work. One thing that really grabbed my interest was Mr. Woodruff’s (head of facilities) handmade skateboards. He made wooden skateboards with each of his kids: one with blue wheels and dark brown wood stripes, and another with green wheels and pink-hued wood stripes down the front.

Another piece that stood out to me was Ms. Docks’ quilt. It had thirty squares, each sewn with a different woodland animal and fabric. My personal favorite animals were the beavers, whose teeth were very cute, but there were many others, including bears, foxes, raccoons, and deer. The square in the center said “Docks” and was stitched over a few colorful mushrooms. It was a great night to showcase the true talent of employees from around campus.

The Staff Art Show was an amazing chance for the Govs community to see a different side of the people who make this place what it is. With so many unique talents on display, the evening was a great reminder that there is always more to know about the people around us.
