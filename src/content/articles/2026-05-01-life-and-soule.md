Whether you know him as the guy with the fancy mustache or as your physics teacher, practically everyone has seen Marcus Soule around campus at some point. And if you’ve ever had him for a teacher, you know how loud and light-hearted he is. But for those who haven’t, here’s all you need to know about him.

Though he never planned to work as an educator, Mr. Soule began teaching early. After a poor test grade on a high school chemistry test, he began tutoring another student as a way to get credit back. He continued tutoring throughout his time studying electrical engineering at the University of Maine, but he didn’t fully expect to go into education until after an especially bad engineering internship.

This internship saw him working at a dairy factory with hours so long he had to get a room at the hotel across the street. While the commute was unbeatable, he decided that he didn’t want to spend his life working in some soulless, thankless job, so he chose to go into teaching. His first true teaching job began in 2005 at a middle school in Lawrence, Massachusetts.

Shortly after joining Govs in 2015, Mr. Soule created an engineering program to meet the desire of many students. He also began coaching the robotics team, which he continues to lead to this day. As of now, he teaches honors engineering, physics, and, if he is asked nicely, chemistry.

Anyone who has been in one of his classes is well aware of his puns and self deprecating jokes. From absence sheets signed “Jeff McScience” to *Soule*\-utions sheets for practice problems, Mr. Soule is great at bringing a smile to the banal affair that school often is. His comedy lights up the classroom every day.

As I can attest, he is loved by nearly all of his students for his patient and engaging methods, kindness, and especially his humor. He is, by far, the teacher I will miss most from Govs next year.

P.S. Don’t worry, he’ll still be teaching here next year.
