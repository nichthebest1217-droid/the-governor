Last year, the Govs girls varsity lacrosse team went undefeated in ISL play and advanced to the ISL championship for the second year in a row. Coming off of an impressive season, the question now is whether the team can repeat that success and bring the championship to Byfield.

A defining moment of that season came when we defeated Nobles 13-9 in regular season play, breaking their six-year undefeated streak in the ISL. In a league as prestigious as the ISL, that win made a big statement. However, when we met them again in the ISL championship, Govs fell short, losing 7-9, allowing them to win their third championship in a row. In such a competitive league, matchups like Nobles will demand even greater focus and preparation. We have hopes to redeem ourselves, and beat them this year. Thayer and Tabor also look strong for the season.

Despite last season’s success, this year’s team looks very different after losing 11 seniors, leaving a very young roster. The roster consists of 20 players, with only three returning seniors, including captain Molly Vana ‘26 (committed to Harvard) and eight returning juniors, including captain Lucy McCormick ‘27.

Although losing key seniors presents challenges, it also creates opportunities for new leaders to step up and for the team to grow stronger together. With many new players stepping into larger roles, one of the team’s biggest challenges this season will be learning the intensity and expectations of the program. Beyond skill, success will depend on how quickly our players can adjust to the fast pace, competitive mindset, and high standards that have defined Govs lacrosse. Despite this, Govs lacrosse remains focused on its goals. As Lucy explained, “because we lost so much of our team last year, the plan is to build the team camaraderie and translate that onto the field.” The team’s goal is to make it to the championship game for the third year in a row.

If we continue building the chemistry we’ve focused on and stay committed to our goals, another championship run is well within reach. This season will ultimately come down to resilience, teamwork, and the ability to rise to the moment.
