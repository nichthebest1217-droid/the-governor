The Dummer Cup is one of the most attended weekend events at Govs every year. From the spikeball tournament to the three-legged race, I saw strong participation, representation from every grade, and great turnout from all dorms and day student teams.

Gezi Nkugwa ‘27 compared this year’s Dummer Cup energy to last year. “It was better this year, especially in Perkins; I think we had the whole dorm participate.” In just four years, the event has become one of Govs’ main traditions. Gezi said, “Personally, I think it is a super fun tradition, and I like competing with other dorms.” Dummer Cup is fun for the school community because it brings out students’ competitive spirit in productive ways.

One of the best moments in the Dummer Cup comes at the start of the event, when all the dorms converge in the field house parking lot. It's fun to see all the dorms’ banner themes and get hyped together as a community. When all the dorms came together, you could see our school’s competitive nature. Everyone pretended to be enemies to win every event.

This year’s Dummer Cup certainly felt like it had better energy than last year. Competitions included wall sitting, spikeball, bagbiting, musical chairs (see picture on page 6), and other categories, and all of these activities helped build a positive vibe and a strong community at Govs. Win or lose, I saw students having fun at every event.

Events like the Dummer Cup are very positive for the school community. The more people participate, the closer we get as a school, and the more fun we all have at events like this. I am excited for future weekend events like the black box dances, and I hope students will come out and join the fun!
