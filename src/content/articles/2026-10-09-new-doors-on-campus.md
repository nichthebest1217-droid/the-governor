With this year’s new door-locking system, I have witnessed teachers and students stuck behind doors and unable to get through at times of day when punctuality is key. With all the new rules and regulations around campus, adding more factors for being late to class isn’t ideal for students trying to succeed. But the door system is a necessary new reality. Maybe the problem doesn’t lie in the new system but in people who are scared of change.

As summer closed, students and faculty returned to campus and a whole new way of navigating the door entries for the buildings. Our Student IDs no longer allow us into buildings; it is a new app on our phones that lets us tap to open doors. Although a new app and new technology sounds fantastic for a school that's been around since the 1700s, it has its upsides and downsides.

The new app allows the school to have more control over access to the buildings in order to keep the campus secure. Mr. McGonagill explains, “Change can be scary, but from a security standpoint, the improved technology will definitely benefit the community in the long run.”

On the other side, Drew Giguere, ‘27, believes that the new app “takes away a sense of privacy because it tracks you wherever you are going.” From a student perspective, the app could make us feel like we are being monitored too closely and eliminate any sense of freedom we had before. But it allows the school to know where we are at all times and to lock down the buildings when there is a threat to campus. For new students, this added to the challenge of learning their way around a new campus.

Cole Cournoyer ‘28 said, “It’s definitely hard to get into buildings, especially transitioning from my old school. Sometimes my phone is dead, or I’m by myself and the system is having trouble while I’m trying to get into the dorm or buildings. It can be frustrating having to wait or figuring it out.” Govs is trying to encourage us to be less dependent on our phones, but needing them on standby at all times contradicts this idea.

There are certainly both positive and negative views of the new door lock system. The app’s functionality definitely needs work, as it has glitches. The Govs community just needs to adjust to the change because the app’s intentions are good. The minor glitches that make the student body uncomfortable will be fixed in the future, and we will all benefit from a safer environment.
