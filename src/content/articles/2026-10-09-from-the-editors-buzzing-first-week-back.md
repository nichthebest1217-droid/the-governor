Full of preseason practices, orientation activities, and new faces arriving on campus, the first weeks back from summer break are meant to bring the school together and kick off the school year. These weeks were buzzing with excitement for the year ahead!

Unity Day has always been a keystone event at the start of the school year. It is a day to connect with peers, enjoy a unique location, and tackle challenges with an adventurous spirit. The freshmen visited Newburyport, rode a boat, and bonded in the ocean breeze. The sophomores geared up at Take Flight in Kittery and took on three different rope courses. The juniors trod Mt. Agamenticus, leaving footprints on dirt paths and in the shade of trees; they pushed towards the summit with both strong mental and physical stamina. Seniors headed south to the Ipswich River and kayaked 9.7 chaotic but fun miles; they all came back exhausted and sunburned, but the day was full of laughter and pride.

Square Dance on Friday was PACKED! Students across all four grades showed out to the Rink parking lot to do-si-do and promenade their hearts out. Students jumped, danced, and ran frantically in circles to old-timey folk songs, even tapping into their childhood roots with the classic Cotton-Eye Joe routine.

On Saturday night, Porter Field was blown up with several inflatables courtesy of Ms. Gargano. These inflatable games included an obstacle course, fighting area, Velcro jumpsuits and a Velcro wall (imagine what happened there), and more! It was an opportunity to watch students smash into inflatable objects and absolutely eat dirt, and it was hilarious. Both square dancing and inflatables were major successes, and students are already looking forward to their return next year!

Our first official day back included familiar and new faces, all dressed up. Some say we should wear formal dress more often, while others say we shouldn’t wear it at all. What do you think? The week began with our first real day of classes and ended with the Dummer Cup. Ingham has the lead in points so far. You can hear more about the Dummer Cup in Owen’s article on the next page, where he speaks with students and faculty about how great last Friday night was.

Tuesday was particularly fun for the seniors; they departed from campus for Senior Sunrise. Arriving at 5:50 a.m., they made it to the beach just in time. StuCo made great photo frames, the deans provided donuts, and there was plenty of good company. They enjoyed taking photos, hanging out with friends, and watching the sun turn orange. One could say it couldn’t have been better, but every bus wanted to stop at Dunkin on the way back!

This action-packed opening week of school set a high-energy tone, promising a great school year ahead. Welcome back, Govs!!
