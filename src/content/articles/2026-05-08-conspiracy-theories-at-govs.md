Everyone has heard of the big conspiracies: the moon landing was fake, the earth is flat, and lizards are running the government. The internet is full of conspiracy theories, some more unbelievable than others, and yet they continue to become popular. But the mainstream media is hiding the truth behind conspiracy theories. These secrets go much deeper than the global scale. Much deeper. In fact, Govs students are surrounded by many dark secrets. Here are some global conspiracies that can be seen on the Governor’s campus.

**Conspiracy 1: Bigfoot.** For decades there have been sightings of a large creature roaming the woods. Scientists have dismissed Bigfoot as a myth, a tall tale, a figment of one’s imagination. But still, sightings continue to happen. Lurking deep in the woods of the Alfond Center is a tall figure that no one has been able to identify. Students on their daily walk to Alfond feel a sinister presence. If one ventures alone on the Alfond path at night they can hear the frightening roar of something evil. “I thought it was a person,” said one traumatized senior. “It stood there in the woods staring at me with its red glowing eyes.” Some say it’s a hoax. I say The Governors Academy is haunted by Bigfoot!

**Conspiracy 2: Weather.** You may or may not have heard the allegations that the mastermind behind chemically engineering the weather is none other than Bill Gates. Rumors suggest that he controls the weather to serve his own purposes. Does this sound similar? Who is a high up operative at The Governor’s Academy who manipulates the weather? Just think: in the week leading up to a predicted snow storm in which the students would be given a day off, the weather took a turn for the better. Coincidence? Sources have reported that Dr. Quimby has been spotted standing at a window on stormy days, laughing wildly and looking up into the sky with a satisfied expression.

**Conspiracy 3: Secret Society.** Around the world, people believe in groups like the Illuminati, full of powerful elites running the world. This might sound like a fairy tale, but Govs has its own secret organization. I should know because I was part of it. One day, standing in the dining hall, I was approached by a senior who handed me a letter saying to come to the chapel for a secret meeting. I didn’t think much about it until I entered the chapel and found a select group of people in costume. I had to offer a sacrifice before I signed my name in a book. But don’t tell anyone because it is a secret!

The Governors Academy isn’t just any ordinary school, it is a utopia of mystery and hidden knowledge. I urge whoever reads this to stay aware of the secrets that lie within the walls of The Governors Academy, because who knows what will happen next…
