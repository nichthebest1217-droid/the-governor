If you’ve been to the weight room this year, you might notice it looks very different from previous years. Over the summer, the weight room has undergone some changes, both to the room itself and to the programs that take place. Leading these changes is the new Strength and Conditioning coach, Ms. O’Connell, known as Coach KK, class of 2021.

One of the biggest changes to the weight room has been the new equipment and layout. The bottom floor of the weight room has been rearranged and now contains all the squat racks and lifting equipment, while the second floor contains treadmills and bikes. In an interview with Coach KK, she said: “This makes the weight room more functional and can have a better flow when there are large groups of people.” In addition, the weight room has added a lot of new equipment, such as three new trap bars, barbells from Merrimack College, resistance bands, and minibands.

Another big change is the new and improved strength and conditioning afternoon program. Unlike previous years, when the program was just open gym time, this year it is more structured. Athletes in the program train together, split into two groups—intro and advanced—with three lifting sessions a week and two speed and agility sessions. Coach KK said, “Having structured programming that allows students to progress throughout the 9-10 weeks can make them stronger and better prepared for their upcoming seasons.” This afternoon program is open to all sophomores, juniors, and seniors.

Coach KK has also been helping varsity teams with team lifts. In an interview with girls varsity soccer captain Hannah Babo ‘27, she described mobility work and four sets of different exercises. The time in the weight room is only thirty minutes; however, Hannah said, “It will help us get faster and get stronger, and it's a very quick 30 minutes. It's very efficient, and Coach KK definitely helps us get better.”

Another priority in the weight room is keeping the space clean and well maintained. In past years, the weight room has been left a mess; however, Coach KK plans on enforcing expectations by “holding people accountable who don’t follow the rules.” Additionally, Coach KK said, “If we don't clean up our space, then we can’t use the weight room — but reminders do go a long way.”

Over the summer, Coach KK has put a substantial amount of time and effort into changing the weight room and creating a new strength program. Her goal for the year is to “have everyone have a chance to learn a little bit more about exercising and find time to enjoy it.”
