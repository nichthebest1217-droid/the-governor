by Toby Grenier ‘26

The Boys Varsity lacrosse team has put together a strong start to the season, currently holding a 3-1 record in the ISL and a 4–3 overall record. While the numbers show a solid league start, they don’t fully capture the ups and downs the team has experienced. After facing early adversity, the team has begun to find its rhythm, building momentum at the right point in the season to position itself for a strong finish.

The boys in cardinal set the tone with an impressive 15–8 victory in their home opener against Phillips Exeter. The offense clicked early, and the team played with confidence on both ends of the field. However, the following stretch proved challenging. Back-to-back overtime losses against Noble & Greenough and Middlesex were tough to swallow. The Nobles game, in particular, highlighted the team’s resilience as they clawed back from a 4-goal deficit in the fourth quarter.

Instead of letting those losses define their season, the team responded with a strong bounce-back stretch. Picking up their first two league wins over Groton and St. George’s showed the team’s ability to address their weaknesses. The energy in the locker room shifted after a huge 14–10 win against Belmont Hill, the two-time defending ISL Champions. It was a statement win against a strong opponent and showed glimpses of what’s to come for the team.

A major factor for the team’s success has been standout performances from key players. On offense, Tripp Schuwerk ‘26 has led the way with 20 goals and 1 assist, consistently finding the back of the net. Captain Johnny McDonald ‘26 has also been a major contributor with 17 goals and 4 assists, while Captain Ford Sullivan ‘26 adds balance with 10 goals and 8 assists. This strong senior trio has given the team its offensive firepower and the ability to score in a variety of ways.

On the defensive end, Captain Cooper Schuhwerk ‘26 has been a leader, anchoring the defensive unit and setting the tone with physical, disciplined play. Between the pipes, Owen Gaebel ‘27 has been a reliable constant, keeping the team in games when it matters most. Together, the defense has shown clear improvement as a unit and looks to build on its success. 

Beyond individual performances, the team’s biggest strengths are its scoring depth and defensive growth. The sideline energy and support from players like Andrew Melly ‘26 and Nate Jackson ‘27 have helped create a stronger, more bonded group.

Looking ahead, the team will round out its season facing tough matchups against St. Sebastian’s and Milton Academy. These games will be key tests as the team continues to build on its recent success. Overall, the team’s goal remains clear: Improving day in and day out to put themselves in a position to compete for an ISL championship and hoist the trophy in late May.
