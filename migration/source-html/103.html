by Tyler Passero ‘26

As everybody eased back into school life the Tuesday after spring break, students were shocked to notice that their beloved dining hall had some new and improved components. The dining hall staff was hard at work over spring break making many new changes to the recipes, dining materials, layout, and much more.

For starters, the layout for the buffet area changed, making it feel more like a five star hotel breakfast area. Instead of having the traditional  silverware holder, there is now a  fancy counter where the silverware sits in black baskets. There are also new plates to serve the pizza and new cookers to hold the rice. Near the area where the burgers and hotdogs are served, there are heat lamps that keep the food warm and fresh. Overall, the new changes provide easier maneuverability through the servery area to help people get their food quicker.

When you head into the dining area, you will see plenty of new aspects as well. For starters, the cereal area had a complete overhaul, changing the cereal dispensers and the different kinds of cereals. The dining hall staff also added an area where you can get peanut butter, jelly, butter, cream cheese, and bagels. And finally, there are now plastic cups that hold a little more liquid and prevent glass shattering. 

The response to the new changes has been mostly ambivalent. Jason ‘26 says he “Likes the new changes because all of the nicer dispensers and bottles help prevent mess on the floor and on clothes as well.” Vincent ‘27 feels that  a lot of food options have been cut, including the special protein options provided by the burgers and chicken. He also thinks that the portions of food have become smaller. 

Overall, the new changes to the dining hall have impacted the Govs community by making the dining experience better.
