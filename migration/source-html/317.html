by Alex Hughey ‘27

If you ask me, spring at Govs is undefeated. Maybe I am biased, as a member of the girls lacrosse team, but once the weather warms up and students and faculty return from spring break, campus feels alive in a way no other season compares to. It is the time everyone starts looking ahead, a countdown to prom, graduation, and to finally summer! 

Warm weather slowly replaces winter coats with light sweaters, and spring colors. The snow finally melts, revealing green grass we forgot was even there. Flowers bloom behind Frost and along campus walkways, and suddenly everything feels lighter. The energy on campus shifts. More laughter on the paths between classes, more students studying outside on Stu patio, or eating lunch outside Phillips, embracing the sunshine. All reasons to stay outside just a little longer before heading home or to the dorms. 

Spring sports bring a different kind of excitement. From lacrosse games under the bright afternoon sun, to baseball and softball doubleheaders, the sidelines are full again after a long and cold winter of hibernation. Even if you are not an athlete, there’s something about cheering for your classmates for one final season that makes campus feel united. The spring play adds its own spotlight, filling the theater with exciting drama. Usually written by one of our students, the play gives students another reason to gather as a community to support and celebrate each other’s talent.

Then there are the traditions that make Govs spring unforgettable: Assassin, Twilight Softball, and prom. These moments make us realize how lucky we are to have the community we have created. They are a way to keep a light hearted, fun yet competitive spirit to campus. These memories will end up in camera rolls and alumni will look back on and reminisce about the joy it brought. As commencement weekend approaches, seniors start to realize that the “lasts” are happening in real time: last game, last class, last Dhall rot, last chapel talk or convocation as students.

And somehow, even with finals approaching, the countdown to summer keeps everyone motivated. There’s a shared understanding across campus: we made it through winter. Spring at Govs isn’t just a season, it’s a celebration of everything we’ve worked toward all year.
