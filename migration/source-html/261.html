by Andrew Melly ‘26

One of the most unique parts of The Governor’s Academy is the PA block. It’s a two-hour period from 11:00 to 1:00 that gives students who don’t play in the ensembles a much-needed break in the middle of their day. During this time, students can grab lunch, catch up on homework, or take part in activities that they might not otherwise have time for in their regular schedule. Compared to a typical study hall, PA Block feels more relaxed and flexible, reducing stress and giving students more control over how they spend their time.

One of the biggest benefits of PA Block is the opportunity to eat lunch without feeling rushed.  Many other schools have lunch blocks that don’t overlap, making it impossible to see friends outside of structured blocks. In the Phillips Dining Hall, students can sit with friends, take a break from classes, and go back for seconds to recharge before the rest of the day. Having that extra time makes lunch feel more relaxed and social, where students can actually unwind, talk, and take a mental break.

It also provides time to get homework done during the productive midday hours. Many students finish a good chunk of their work during these hours, allowing them more time to relax in the evening. Students can choose a comfortable space that works best for them, whether in the library, the student center, or in their dorm rooms.

Another great part of PA Block is that it allows students to explore interests like art or other creative classes that may not fit into their daily schedules. PA helped me find my love for ceramics when it would otherwise not have fit into my schedule. This class meets twice a week during the PA block and has helped me develop an artistic outlet that was nonexistent until I was 17. Others sing in the choirs or play their instruments in the orchestra and jazz band during this time. This adds variety to the school day and gives students a chance to try something new.

Overall, PA Block is both productive and relaxing. Some of my favorite memories of PA block are going on senior leave and walking around Newburyport, playing spikeball on Ingham lawn, discussing in immense detail the ingredients of Lisa’s seven-layer bars, and annoying Chris about a package that was supposed to be delivered weeks ago. PA block sparks new loves, relieves stress, and builds bonds here at The Governors Academy.
