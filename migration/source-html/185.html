by Tyler Passero ‘26

If you have played a sport or spent any time in the gym at Govs, you have experienced the ancient temple that is the fieldhouse. Many students believe that the fieldhouse is due for renovations in order to improve athletes’ enjoyment of the space. But what is the history behind the fieldhouse? And if it were to be changed, what exactly should be changed? 

Surprisingly, the fieldhouse is a fairly recent building on campus, having been built in 1992. Originally dreamed up by Carl A. Pescosolido, the fieldhouse was designed with the intention of being a positive and long lasting space for the Govs community to come together. And while this has often been the case, the fieldhouse is definitely not perfect.

The fieldhouse has many positives. It has three full courts, allowing for multiple teams to practice at once and for many people to play basketball, tennis, volleyball, or other sports. Additionally, the Alumni Gym is in great shape and continues to be enjoyed by many students at Govs. And finally, the training room is a spacious area that gives athletes room to stretch and receive the help they need if they are injured.

However, there are several negatives that some would argue outweigh the positives. For starters, the flooring of the courts is very worn down and hard, leading to many knee, shin, and ankle injuries. In addition, the track in the fieldhouse has become impractical and at times dangerous due to the space not being big enough for many runners at a time and the turns being too sharp. Many students have also complained that the weight room is too small and does not have enough machines or space. Finally, there are a few maintenance issues that require attention, including inadequate lighting in some rooms and roof leaks that result in water dripping onto the track and basketball courts.

 When deciding which building to renovate next,I believe the fieldhouse should be the first building that should be renovated. We should expand the weight room so that more people can use machines, and we should fix the roof, flooring, and lighting. In addition, we should also improve the track so that it is viable to host track meets. The fieldhouse is a great building on campus, but I believe that it needs to be severely renovated in order to become an even more pivotal part of the Govs community and live up to its true potential.
